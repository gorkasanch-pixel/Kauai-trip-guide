Getting Around

4 routes
Kauai has no functioning rideshare network — the rental car is the only way around. There's no road that circles the island: the Nā Pali Coast blocks it, so the North Shore and West Side are both dead ends you drive back out of. Gas stations cluster in Lihue, Kapaʻa, and Poipu — top off before heading to the North Shore, since there's nothing past Princeville.

Lihue Airport → Koloa Landing Resort

~15–20 MIN · HWY 51 → HWY 520 (TREE TUNNEL ROAD)

Pick up the rental car at the airport, then head south on Highway 520 — the "Tree Tunnel," a mile-long canopy of eucalyptus planted in 1911, marks the approach into Koloa town just before Poipu.

Resort → Waimea Canyon State Park

~50–60 MIN · HWY 50 WEST, THEN HWY 550 UP
  1. Take Highway 50 (Kaumualii Hwy) west through Hanapepe and Waimea town.
  2. Just past mile marker 23, turn onto Highway 550 (Waimea Canyon Drive).
  3. Climb up to the lookouts — Waimea Canyon Lookout first, Puu Ka Pele and Kalalau Lookout further up.
Entrance fee: $5/person plus $10 parking for non-residents, payable by card at the lookout. Go early — clouds tend to roll in by early afternoon.

Resort → Kapaʻa / Coconut Coast

~30 MIN · HWY 520 → HWY 56 NORTH
  1. Head north on Highway 520 to Lihue, then pick up Highway 56 (Kuhio Hwy).
  2. Follow it north past Wailua to Kapaʻa — the Coconut Coast.
Heads up: the stretch through Kapaʻa is notorious for traffic locals call the "Kapaʻa Crawl" — avoid the drive right around 4–5:30 PM if you can.

Resort → Hanalei / Princeville

~75–90 MIN · HWY 520 → HWY 56 ALL THE WAY NORTH
  1. Same route as Kapaʻa, just kept going — Highway 56 continues north through Kilauea to Princeville and down into Hanalei.
  2. Past Hanalei, the road narrows and crosses several one-lane bridges — the first car to arrive goes first, and locals cross 5–7 cars at a time before yielding to the other side.
Haʻena State Park (Kēʻē Beach / Kalalau trailhead): requires a parking reservation booked in advance at gohaena.com — you can't just drive up.

Day by Day

6 days
  • 11:48 AM

    Land at Lihue

    Car pickup at 12:48 PM, then head to Poipu.

  • ~1:30 PM

    First taste of Poipu

    Too early to check in — a low-key first stop at Spouting Horn or Shipwreck Beach eases everyone in after a long travel day.

  • 4:00 PM

    Check in

    Koloa Landing Resort, One-Bedroom Island-View Villa.

  • Evening

    First dinner

    Keoki's Paradise or Eating House 1849 — both an easy 5-minute drive, festive first-night options.

  • Morning

    Waimea Canyon

    Go early before clouds roll in — the drive itself, up Highway 550, is half the experience.

  • Midday

    Lunch on the way back

    Pilina Coffee or a stop in Hanapepe town for something quick.

  • Afternoon

    Poipu Beach Park

    Calm, reef-protected swimming — a good decompression after the canyon drive.

  • Morning

    Head north early

    It's a real drive — leaving by 8–9 AM beats both traffic and the crowds at North Shore parking lots.

  • Late Morning

    Kilauea Point & Hanalei Bay

    Lighthouse and seabird colony first (reservation required), then Hanalei Bay for a swim.

  • Lunch

    Hanalei town

    Wake Up Delicious for a casual plate, or Tahiti Nui for the famous Mai Tai and pizza.

  • Afternoon

    Anini or Tunnels Beach

    Anini is calmer and better for younger kids; Tunnels has better snorkeling if the ocean's calm.

  • Morning

    Wailua Falls

    No hike required — an easy drive-up lookout, good for the kids' ages.

  • Late Morning

    Optional: Wailua River kayak

    Kayak Wailua's guided trip to Secret Falls includes a rainforest hike — better suited if the kids are up for it; otherwise the falls lookout alone is plenty for the morning.

  • Lunch

    Kapaʻa

    Tony's Catch (food truck, fish tacos) or Kauai Myanmar Food & Sushi.

  • Afternoon

    Wailua Shave Ice

    A Kapaʻa institution — go generous on the toppings.

  • Morning

    McBryde & Allerton Garden

    Guided tram tour through the botanical garden, a few minutes from the resort — book ahead.

  • Afternoon

    Old Koloa Town

    Shops, food trucks, and a final round of shave ice at JoJo's.

  • Evening

    Sunset dinner

    Beach House Restaurant — timed for sunset on the last full evening.

  • Morning

    Relaxed last morning

    Poipu Beach Park one more time before checkout.

  • 11:00 AM

    Check out

    Car isn't due back until 7:10 PM — no rush.

  • Afternoon

    Drive toward Lihue

    Time for one more stop — Salt Pond Beach Park is a good on-the-way option.

  • Evening

    Last dinner + departure

    Duke's Kauai on Kalapaki Beach, right in Lihue — drop the car at 7:10 PM ahead of the 9:10 PM flight.
